- [Feature] Optionally mirror the SCORM zip into the course contentstore on upload and rehydrate the unpacked tree from the contentstore on first access. This makes blocks survive OLX course export/import without breaking the extraction path keyed by `sha1(usage_key)` introduced in PR #71. Disabled by default; enable with `XBLOCK_SETTINGS["ScormXBlock"]["CONTENTSTORE_SYNC_ENABLED"] = True`. (by @ali.abbas02)
